Not an SB 53 copy — SB 53's whistleblower chapter only, minus the published safety framework, minus the transparency report, minus critical-incident reporting, plus an anonymous internal reporting channel due January 1, 2027, and with the penalty cut from $1,000,000 to $1,000 per violation.
