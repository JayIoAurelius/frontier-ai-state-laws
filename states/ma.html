G.L. c. 93M s. 1 (proposed), definition of 'Catastrophic risk'
Tightens CA

Catastrophic risk: 50 people or $1B, on the same four limbs — with the threshold inclusive
“Catastrophic risk”, a foreseeable and material risk that a frontier developer’s development, storage, use or deployment of a frontier model [a foundation model trained using more than 10^26 integer or floating-point operations (c. 93M s. 1)] will materially contribute to the death of, or serious injury to, not less than 50 people or not less than $1,000,000,000 in damage to, or loss of, property arising from a single incident involving a frontier model that: (i) provides expert-level assistance in the creation or release of a chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear weapon; (ii) engages in conduct with no meaningful human oversight, intervention or supervision that is either a cyberattack or, if the conduct had been committed by a human, would constitute the crime of murder, assault, extortion or theft, including theft by false pretense; or (iii) evades the control of its frontier developer or user; provided, however, that “catastrophic risk” shall not include a foreseeable and material risk from: (A) information that a frontier model outputs if the information is otherwise publicly accessible in a substantially similar form from a source other than a foundation model; (B) lawful activity of the federal government; or (C) harm caused by a frontier model in combination with other software if the frontier model did not materially contribute to the harm.

(c 1/2)(1) A large frontier developer [a frontier developer whose group annual gross revenues exceed $500,000,000 (c. 93M s. 1)] shall clearly and conspicuously publish on its internet website a risk report that provides an overall assessment of the catastrophic risks posed by: (i) any frontier models the large frontier developer deploys externally; and (ii) any internally deployed frontier models with capabilities that materially exceed those of any frontier model [a foundation model trained using more than 10^26 integer or floating-point operations (c. 93M s. 1)] that frontier developer has externally deployed. The delta

Substantively the SB 53 definition, same four limbs and same three carve-outs. Tagged TIGHTENS_CA on one narrow, textually plain parameter: MA reaches risk of harm to 'not less than 50 people or not less than $1,000,000,000', where SB 53 reaches 'more than 50 people or more than one billion dollars' — so a risk pitched at exactly 50 deaths or exactly $1B is inside the Massachusetts definition and outside California's. The practical difference is marginal and a reader may fairly read this cell as MATCHES. Contingent on enactment: S.3228 is the Senate-engrossed text; the House non-concurred 7/30/2026 and conference committees were appointed in both branches.
